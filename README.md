
# Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Interesting facts about my favorite movie-Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018) is a groundbreaking animated feature produced by **Columbia Pictures** and **Sony Pictures Animation**.
- It introduced the **"Spider-Verse"** concept to the mainstream, focusing on Miles Morales as he learns to navigate his new powers alongside other Spider-People from alternate dimensions.
- The film used a unique hybrid of CGI and hand-drawn techniques. Animators applied "line work" over 3D models to mimic the look of a printed comic book.
- The movie utilizes Ben-Day dots, halftones, and chromatic aberration (color fringing) to create depth and texture, moving away from the "motion blur" typically seen in 3D films.
- To give the characters a distinct "stuttery" comic feel, Miles was often animated "on twos" (12 frames per second) while more experienced characters were animated "on ones" (24 frames per second), visually representing    his initial lack of coordination.
